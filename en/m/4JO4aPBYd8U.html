French Dance Mix Céline Dion & Don Diablo

Igor Blaska is at the controls of this French-flavoured dance mix. Céline Dion opens with a remix of “Pour que tu m'aimes encore”, Magic System picks things up with “Vida Loca”, Don Diablo brings “Little Lies”, and Céline returns for “A New Day” with Sebastian Ingrosso. For dancing from the very start of the evening.
