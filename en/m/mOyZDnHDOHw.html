Night Vibe DJ Set French Pop 80s

A late-night set in French, between synths and nostalgia: Jean-Jacques Goldman with Il suffira d'un signe, Sylvie Vartan as Disco queen, Caramel and Boule Noire. Ideal for stretching out the evening or driving late, when Regrets asks not to go home alone.

The night stretches out, the asphalt rolls by and the first notes pierce the darkness like a flash of pure neon. Dive back into the raw intensity of an era when every chorus was a promise of eternal freedom. Find the best tracks of 80s French pop and pop-funk in this exclusive DJ Set. Between forgotten gems and must-have classics like Jean-Jacques Goldman or Sylvie Vartan, this megamix is made for your parties and your car rides.
