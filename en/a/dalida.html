Dalida: mixes and remixes

39 plays in 13 mixes · every song links to the exact minute

Photo: Brown/Rouget/DALLE, Public domain (Wikimedia Commons)

Born in Cairo in 1933, Dalida spanned the eras, from 1960s French pop to the disco of "Laissez-moi danser" (1979): few catalogues lend themselves so well to remixes.

In CyberBob's mixes, Dalida often comes back in remixed versions, somewhere between disco, chill and party vibes. In “Dalida Remix Show - From La Feria to Soleil”, you'll find La Feria (Axel Vicious Remix) and Mamy Blue (Dj Pantelis Remix). And in “Dalida's Greatest Hits Remix”, you can listen to Mourir sur scène with Bon Entendeur or Bang Bang (MIQA Remix).
