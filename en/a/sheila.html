Sheila: mixes and remixes

20 plays in 7 mixes · every song links to the exact minute

Photo: Erio TAC France- Perpignan, CC BY-SA 3.0 (Wikimedia Commons)

A 1960s French pop idol, Sheila went disco in the late 1970s with "Spacer", produced by Chic.

Sheila has a well-deserved place in CyberBob's mixes. In « Sheila's Epic Spacer Nights Remix Will Make You Dance », you'll find Spacer with B. Devotion in the David Kust Extended Remix and Comme Aujourd'hui (Be My Baby) in the DJ RICO Re Edit. « Sheila Remix Revolution » gets you dancing to Tu es le Soleil and Gloria in the DJ EDDY Redrum, or to Les Gondoles à Venise with Ringo in the DJ EDDY DouceFranceMix.
