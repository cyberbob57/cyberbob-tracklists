Anton Wick: remixes

4 remixes in 3 mixes · every song links to the exact minute

A French DJ and producer born in 1980 and originally from Perpignan, Anton Wick has been DJing since he was 15 and was spotted by Laurent Wolf in 2007. On the dance side, he made official remixes for Michel Sardou, Tal and Dany Brillant: those are the ones you hear in CyberBob's mixes, with Femme 2010, Rien n'est parfait and Suzette.

Anton Wick signs 4 remixes in 3 CyberBob in The Mix mixes, including Femme 2010, Rien N'est Parfait, Suzette 2011.

The artists they remix most here: Michel Sardou, Dany Brillant, Tal. The originals range from 1991 to 2012.
